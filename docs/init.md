## User personas
1) Employee: somebody who is employed by Beachside Racetrack.
    - Safety Official: an employee who is responsible for controlling the race and ensuring safety. 
    - Lap-line Observer: an employee who is responsible for recording when cars cross the lap line. This role can be performed by any employee, and does not require specialist training. 
    - Flag Bearer: an employee who stands at specified positions around the circuit. They use different coloured flags to communicate safety instructions to race drivers. 
2) Guest: any person who is present at Beachside Racetrack, but is not an employee.
     -Race driver: a guest who will take part in a race as a non-professional race driver.
     - Spectator: a guest who watches a race, or is interested in comparing driver performances.
